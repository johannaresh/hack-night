How this works

Live flight is computed by dronegym.runner.EpisodeRunner — the same env used in training — then animated on the canvas. WORLD is the isometric view (cyan trail, red front arms, yellow nose, orange target); FPV CAMERA shows the target's bounding box in the drone's-eye frame plus the stick channels; ATTITUDE is pure orientation.

The policy was trained on freestyle_5inch; other airframes show how it transfers (imperfectly) to different physics.
